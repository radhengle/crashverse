# Composer / music sourcing options (for PM decision)
All costs are **ESTIMATES** from the public sources linked below (USD). They are not quotes. Current v1 decision: no paid contracts, so placeholder music only.

Scope assumed: about 14 Ch1/launch tracks (menu, map, 4 islands, Tidewarden, Titan, Titan arena layers, Season 1 hub, stingers), roughly 20–25 finished minutes including stems.

| Option | How it works | Cost estimate | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Indie freelancer, per finished minute | Pay per minute of final music | Indie median **$407/min**, average $618/min, most common $400/min (GameSoundCon 2025 survey). That's about $8k–15k for 20–25 min | Original identity, stems built for adaptive layers | Highest cost; contract and IP negotiation |
| B. Indie freelancer, flat rate per track / package | Fixed fee per track or for the whole soundtrack | Same survey basis: 45% of indie composers don't charge per minute. Expect a similar total; negotiate per track (≈ $400–1,000 per 1–2 min loop, derived from the per-minute data) | Predictable budget | Scope creep risk |
| C. Buyout vs. licence (applies to A/B) | Buyout = we own all rights. Licence = composer keeps copyright and grants us usage | Buyout usually costs more than a licence (no public figure checked; ask in quotes) | Buyout: no Content ID/claim issues. Licence: cheaper, composer can sell the soundtrack | Licence: need a Content ID whitelist and to define store-trailer use |
| D. Royalty-free subscription (e.g. Epidemic Sound) | Monthly or yearly plan, royalty-free use of the catalogue | Prices aren't shown in the fetched page text (https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing/). Check live. Game/app use may need Pro/Enterprise | Fast, large catalogue | Not exclusive; licence ties to the subscription period (check terms for apps); not CC0; **excluded under the current no-paid rule** |
| E. AI-free placeholder approach (current) | CC0/royalty-free loops (Kenney Music Jingles CC0, OpenGameArt CC0 music) and in-house sketches, all logged | $0 cash; about 2–4 days to curate, loop-edit and key-match | No spend, unblocks development and playtests | Generic sound, no motif, inconsistent keys; must not ship as the final identity |

Sources (public, checked 16 Oct 2026):
- GameSoundCon Game Audio Industry Survey 2025, freelance composer rates: https://www.gamesoundcon.com/post/gamesoundcon-game-audio-industry-survey-2025 (AAA avg $1,871/min; midcore avg $998; indie avg $618, median $407)
- Survey coverage (654 respondents): https://www.soundonsound.com/news/gamesoundcons-game-audio-2025-survey
- 2023 survey, for the trend: https://www.gamesoundcon.com/post/game-audio-industry-survey-2023
- Epidemic Sound pricing/FAQ: https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing/

## Recommendation
1. **Now:** Option E. Build placeholder music from logged CC0 sources and keep the C/Am key and the bar-exact loop spec so swapping later is a drop-in.
2. **Before soft launch:** if a budget opens, Option A/B with one indie composer, **buyout incl. stems** (C), for the about 14 launch tracks (≈ $8k–15k estimate). Later chapters (provisional) go in separate per-chapter add-on agreements, delivered as downloadable content.
3. Avoid D for the core identity (not exclusive). Use it at most for trailers if the no-paid rule changes.
