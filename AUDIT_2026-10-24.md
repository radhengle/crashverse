## 3. Why builds failed / the APK was bad (root causes, deduplicated)
1. **Nothing reaches main.** The work sits in about 43 PRs stacked 2 to 4 deep, and each one needs 3 approvals. Only 6 have merged, and none of them are gameplay or visual fixes. Every "fix" was built from a side branch, never from main.
2. **No real content in the project.** Main has no URP asset, materials, prefabs, models or audio. The models exist only locally: 59 FBX are committed in `/workspace/wt/3d-modeler-v3` on branch `3d-modeler/models-v3`, but that branch is **not pushed** (only `models-v1` is on the remote). The .blend sources are in `models_src`. The 46 .ogg files are only in PR #32.
3. **The URP tier assets have no owner.** #23, #42 and #43 each try to create them, or test for them, at `Settings/URP`. The rendering tests in #43 and #48 fail with "Quality tier 'Mid' missing", and the world renders magenta.
4. **IL2CPP stripping:** there is no link.xml (#44 is unmerged), so shots hit nothing.
5. **Legacy Input:** it is still used, the axes are missing, and the fix in #23 is conflicting. That causes the exception every frame and the dead taps.
6. **CI that misleads or breaks:**
   - The main APK job is green on an empty game.
   - server-ci on main is red (3 purchase/refund tests) after #1 and #19 were merged while on hold.
   - The emulator gate (#36) reports "0 tests ran".
   - The stacked branches miss files (#51) or break the CompileCheck stubs (`Mathf.Deg2Rad`, #49).
7. **Scope spread:** a lot of effort went into the backend, economy, age-gate compliance and docs (#1, #3, #10, #19, #33: more than 15k lines) while the core loop on the phone stayed broken.
