# CRASHVERSE — Chapter 1 Sunnyville Isles, Levels v1 (rev 5: fun redesign + rulings 7a/8 + gravity ×1.2)
Owner: Level Designer · Branch `level-designer/ch1-levels` (draft PR #13) · Applies Level_Rulings_v1 plus rulings 7a, 8 and PR #15 gravity.

**Data:** `levels/ch1/gen_levels.py` (source) writes `layouts/*.json` and `chapter1_levels.json`, in the loader schema (`ContentModels.cs`). The same files are copied into `Assets/_Project/Resources/Data/`. This doc is generated by `mkdoc.py` from `sim_results.json`.
**Rules:** HP wood 30 / brick 60 / steel 120. Mass 1 / 2.5 / 4. A shot does 60, and a direct hit on a steel joint does 2×. Shockwave does 40 splash, 1 link deep. Splitstrike is 3×50%. **Debris (ruling 7a):** min(60, 0.5 × mass × (v − 2)) × the struck piece's material factor (wood 1.0, brick 1.1, steel 0.8), applied when v > 2 m/s, where v = √(2 · 1.2g · drop) (gravity ×1.2, PR #15). **Ruling 8:** a piece collapses only when all of its supports are gone. Win at ≥0.85 destroyed. Stars = shotsLeft/shots ≥ band. L10 is won by defeat: 8 shots, bands .375/.25, spec shutter (ruling 3).
**Stone (ruling 6):** Indestructible + NonObjective, grey `8A8A8A`, role `island`. It's used only as terrain or piers, never on a hint target or an optimal line (checked by script).

## Levels
### L1. Seagull Perch: T-shaped cliff lookout on one crooked post [Easy]
- Materials: wood · Shots 4 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Aim + fire
- Chain "aha": Post snaps → deck, both side cabins, hut, roof and bell all drop
- Risk/reward: The big roof is a decoy that takes a shot and achieves almost nothing
- Mastery: >=95% destroyed
- Why it's fun: A single shot clears the whole cliff-top, so you get a big payoff in the first second.
- Proof: min 1 shot(s) via post; 3★ needs ≤1; margin 3.
### L2. Leaning Water Tower plus a pump shed on a catwalk [Easy]
- Materials: wood · Shots 4 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Hit supports, not walls
- Chain "aha": Sill → stilts → tank, and the catwalk drags the pump shed down too (domino)
- Risk/reward: The tank lid is the obvious target and it is useless
- Mastery: Topple the tank off the island edge
- Why it's fun: The tank looks like the target, but a low hit topples the tower and takes the shed with it.
- Proof: min 1 shot(s) via sill; 3★ needs ≤1; margin 3.
### L3. Two-Storey Bakery on two wood props [Medium]
- Materials: wood + brick · Shots 5 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Brick needs support loss
- Chain "aha": Two props → shop walls → upper flat, balcony, roof and chimney fold
- Risk/reward: The awning post is a decoy: it holds only the awning
- Mastery: >=95% destroyed
- Why it's fun: Shooting brick feels useless until you knock the props out and two storeys slump at once.
- Proof: min 2 shot(s) via propL, propR; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 3.
### L4. Windmill with a brick annex [Hard]
- Materials: wood + brick · Shots 5 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Collapse chains
- Chain "aha": Beams → drum → cap → top drum → sails, and the annex beam drops the annex (the longest chain so far)
- Risk/reward: Drum faces (role wall) also reach 3★ but fail the no-wall mastery
- Mastery: 3 stars with no wall hits
- Why it's fun: Following the chain through mill and annex is the chapter's first "whoa".
- Proof: min 2 shot(s) via beamL, beamR; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 3.
### L5. Twin cottages on a bridge, with a water-wheel and a lantern string [Breather]
- Materials: wood + brick · Shots 5 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: One shot, two targets
- Chain "aha": Bridge → both cottages, the hanging wheel and the lantern string
- Risk/reward: Brick cottage walls are decoys
- Mastery: Both in 1 shot
- Why it's fun: A relief level: one clean shot and the whole bridge scene tumbles.
- Proof: min 1 shot(s) via bridge; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 4.
### L6. Grain Silo: a tall 3-drum silo on gold steel braces, with a side hopper [Medium]
- Materials: brick + steel · Shots 5 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Steel joints take 2×
- Chain "aha": Two braces → deck → silo drums, steel dome, chute and hopper
- Risk/reward: braceM is a decoy joint (only needed for mastery); the steel dome is a 120-HP trap
- Mastery: Break all 3 joints
- Why it's fun: Two gold joints topple a 10 m silo, so spotting weak points feels clever.
- Proof: min 2 shot(s) via braceL, braceR; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 3.
### L7. Clockwork Tower (M1, Shockwave forced) [Hard]
- Materials: wood + brick + steel · Shots 5 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Joint sequencing
- Chain "aha": Scaffold reveals ring0 → the whole shaft and crown drop as one stack (the crown can land intact)
- Risk/reward: Exposed ring1 is the risk target: it drops only 58%
- Mastery: Crown falls intact
- Why it's fun: A puzzle within a puzzle: find the hidden ring and keep the crown whole.
- Proof: min 2 shot(s) via scaffold, ring0; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 3.
### L8. Market arcade with a clock arch [Breather]
- Materials: all 3 · Shots 5 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Choose loadout (Splitstrike)
- Chain "aha": One split shot breaks 3 keystones → 3 bays, the arch, the steel sign and the clock ripple down
- Risk/reward: The steel sign looks like the target but takes 1 shot for little
- Mastery: 3 stars
- Why it's fun: A power-up showcase where one shot does three shots' work.
- Proof: min 4 shot(s) via key0, splitstrike:key1+key2+key3; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 3.
### L9. Lighthouse + seawall, joined by a catwalk [Hard]
- Materials: all 3 · Shots 6 · Bands 0.6/0.3 · Teaches: Tests everything
- Chain "aha": Ring → lighthouse; deck → seawall, bell tower, then the catwalk falls once both ends are gone
- Risk/reward: The catwalk is a trap: it falls only when both structures go
- Mastery: No power-up used
- Why it's fun: A real exam: two structures, two exact shots, and the catwalk pays off only if you do both.
- Proof: min 2 shot(s) via lhRing, seaDeck; 3★ needs ≤2; margin 4.
### L10. The Tidewarden on a sea-stack (boss) [Boss]
- Materials: brick + steel + wood · Shots 8 · Bands 0.375/0.25 · Teaches: Boss phases + weak points
- Chain "aha": Both shoulders → arms fall off and the chest opens; core ×2 → collapse
- Risk/reward: Shooting into the closed shutter wastes a shot and fails mastery
- Mastery: Defeat the Tidewarden without hitting the closed shutter
- Why it's fun: A boss you read rather than grind: break the shoulders, time the shutter and land two core hits.
- Proof: min 5 shot(s) via shoulderL, shoulderR, core(open), any(closed), core(open); 3★ needs ≤5; margin 3.

## Notes
- L7 crown-intact mastery: with rulings 7a and 8 and gravity ×1.2, the 2-shot line leaves 4/4 crown pieces unbroken, so it's achievable.
- L10 mastery means the shot fired on the closed turn must not hit the shutter (aim at the head or lamp).
- **Repeat the final check in Unity once Physics PR #11 and gravity PR #15 land.**
- The sim is abstract: occlusion assumes the camera is on +z, debris applies on first landing only, and a collapsed piece counts if its base is ≥1 m up. Assumption: the 7a material factor uses the struck piece's material.
