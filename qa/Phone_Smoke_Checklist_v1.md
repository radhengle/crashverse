# Phone Smoke Checklist v1 (dev build 675ca91)
Tick each one; for any ✗ send a screenshot/screen recording + what you tapped.

1. **Install & launch**: APK installs (allow "unknown sources"); icon reads CRASHVERSE; opens without crash.
2. **Cold start**: from tap to first interactive screen in under ~5 s (count it).
3. **Login screens**: guest/Play/Google/Facebook buttons show the official logos; guest entry works.
4. **Map**: Chapter 1 shows 10 levels; L1 unlocked, others locked; scrolling is smooth.
5. **Play L1 – win**: aim, fire, structure breaks; win screen shows stars and score.
6. **Stars**: win with shots left gives more stars (3 shots left of 4 ⇒ 3★).
7. **Play L1 – fail**: run out of shots below 85% ⇒ fail screen; Retry works.
8. **Hint**: fail the same level twice in a row ⇒ Free Hint appears (not after 1 fail; no ad icon).
9. **Pause/resume**: pause mid-shot, resume; physics continues correctly, shot count unchanged.
10. **Rotate & background**: rotate phone; press Home for 30 s and return ⇒ same state, no reset/crash.
11. **Smoothness**: aiming and collapses feel smooth (≈60 FPS); note any stutter, overheating or big battery drain over 10 min.
12. **Offline**: airplane mode ⇒ campaign still plays; online-only features show a clear message, no crash.
13. **No crashes**: play 15 min; note any crash/freeze (time + what you did).
14. **QA tools panel (SEC-02)**: tap ⚙ on the map. In this *dev* build a QA panel may appear (expected); it must **not** appear in the release build.
15. **Sound**: no audio in this build (expected).
