# Game Design Review: Physics Feel Proposal v1 (PR #15)
Reviewer: Game designer. Verdict: **approved with the changes below**. Everything not mentioned is approved as a starting tunable.

- **(a) Time Fracture:** Approved as 200 ticks at 0.25× for moving parts, and used in **every mode**, not only ranked, so it behaves the same everywhere. It still starts on arm (OQ-P3) and ends at 200 ticks or at the fired shot's impact tick. Real-time slow-mo stays forbidden in ranked runs.
- **(b) Gravity ×1.2:** Approved. The aim preview must use the same integrator. Level Designer re-checks Ch1 shot budgets and star bands after the change.
- **(c) New power-ups:** None of them join the launch set. The six power-ups are final (Content Roadmap §2). Heavy shot and Ricochet go on the backlog as candidates for event-only or Season 2 power-ups. Chain lightning becomes a **Neon District level mechanic** (energy conduits on steel), not a power-up.
- **(d) Glass:** Neon District and later only. Not in M1 or Ch1.
- **Impact tiers and hit-stop:** Approved. Hit-stop is presentation only, max 80ms, and turned off by Reduce motion.
- **Materials:** Approved as tunables, including brick overflow (25%, 1 link) and steel's dent, bend and tear stages. Level Designer validates on Ch1.
- **Crack stages and pre-shot stress cracks:** Approved. Good for readability.
- **Staggered collapse:** Approved.
- **Environment Set A** (ice, wind, low gravity, springs, barrels): approved as engine features. They're not used in Ch1, which teaches materials only. Barrels arrive with Chain Reactor in Gearstorm; ice is mainly Crystal Frontier; wind, low gravity and springs are for Ch2+ and events.
- **Set B and the roadmap order:** Fine, with Set B scheduled by PM.
- **Debris damage and collapse rulings:** The source is design/Level_Rulings_v1.md, items #7 and #8.
