# Level Rulings v1 (Chapter 1, answers to Level Designer)
Owner: Game designer. These update Design Pack v1 §1, SG-05 and the M1 contract v0.2 note.

1. **L10 budget and mastery:** Raise the shot budget to **8**. The mastery goal changes to **"Defeat the Tidewarden without hitting the closed shutter"**. A perfect run takes 5 shots: 2 joints, then core, closed, core.
2. **L10 star bands:** 3★ at ≥ 0.375 (3 or more left of 8, so a perfect 5-shot run) and 2★ at ≥ 0.25 (2 left). This replaces the proposed 28%/14%. It's per-level data, as allowed by SG-01.
3. **Phase 2 shutter:** The spec is the intended behaviour. The shutter is **open** when phase 2 starts, and the shot that ended phase 1 doesn't toggle it. It toggles only after each shot fired during phase 2. The current code is a bug for Developer to fix.
4. **M1 / L7 tower:** The 2-shot tuning is approved. Under v0.2, 3★ is a 2-shot clear and 2★ is a 3-shot clear. The v0.1 line "solvable in 3 skilled shots" is retired.
5. **L1 and L2 at 4 shots:** Fine for the tutorial. L1's mastery goal changes to **"≥95% destroyed"**, so it isn't the same as getting 3★. L2 keeps "tank off the island edge".
6. **Unbreakable stone bases:** Approved. Tag them `Indestructible` + `NonObjective`, keep them out of totalObjectiveMass, and give them a distinct look (plain grey stone). This is terrain, not a fourth material. Don't use it to block the intended weak points.
7. **Debris damage:** Intended. Chain collapses depend on it. Spec: debris does damage only above a relative impact speed of 2 m/s, scaled by mass × speed and capped at 60 per collision. Pieces resting against each other do no damage. "Crown intact" on L7 is meant to be hard. If testing shows it can't be done, tune the crown's HP or layout, not the debris rule.
8. **Collapse rule:** A piece with supports collapses only when **all** of its supports (joints or anchors) are gone. A single remaining support holds it, unless that joint breaks from load (break force or torque). This is the ruling Physics quoted.
7a. **Debris damage scale (tuning):** damage = min(60, 0.5 × mass_kg × (relSpeed − 2)), applied only above 2 m/s. Measuring speed above the threshold avoids a sudden jump at 2 m/s. Material multipliers: wood ×1.0, brick ×1.1, steel ×0.8. This replaces the 2.5 placeholder. Example: a 20 kg brick chunk at 5 m/s does 33. It's a starting value: Level Designer checks it on L7's crown and on Ch1, and reports if collisions hit the cap too often.
9. **Glass and stone (PR #25):** Glass is HP 12 and takes debris damage ×1.5, Neon District (Ch3) and later only. Breakable stone is HP 90 and takes debris damage ×0.9. Both are starting values. Breakable stone is a separate material from the `Indestructible` stone bases in ruling #6, which never take damage, and the two must look clearly different.
10. **Debris scale check:** Level Designer's sim found no collision reaching the cap (worst case about 27). Keep 0.5 for Ch1. Revisit after the Unity check once PR #6, #11 and #15 merge.
