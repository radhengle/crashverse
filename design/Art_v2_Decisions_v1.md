# Art v2 Open-Item Decisions v1
Owner: Game designer.

- **06 Level complete, mastery:** The screen shows "Level N Complete" (UX-38), the stars, the shots left, and the level's mastery goal text with a tick or an empty box. Mastery is a separate tick and never changes stars (SG-03). If the level has no mastery goal, that row is hidden. Score is shown as on the win screen (ADD §3), using a realistic value for the level shown.
- **C-08 Daily Titan HP bar:** Approved. The bar shows remaining HP = 10,000 − damage dealt, so it shows the same thing as score = damage. It's marked at 75/50/25% to match the stage breaks (OQ-B3). No hidden HP math: the bar is the score. The Titan's score is still damage + 500 per shot left on kill.
- **UX-37 Live score:** No live score in campaign runs. The HUD shows shots left and % destroyed, with COMBO pops. Score appears only on the win screen. In the Daily Titan, the live HP bar plays that role.
- **SG-37 Star milestones:** Per chapter at 10/20/30★. Ch1 rewards are chests of coins, shards and a cosmetic decal. No gems. See Spec_Gap_Answers_v1.md SG-37. This replaces Art's 30/60/90.
- **Season theme rename:** "Jurassic World" is replaced by **Primeval Peaks**: an original prehistoric theme with fossil cliffs, giant ferns and amber crystals. No dinosaur IP and no copied logos. I didn't pick "Fossil Frontier" because it clashes with "Wrecking Frontier" and "Crystal Frontier".
- **Gems:** earned only in v1 (see Economy_v1_Earned_Gems.md). Milestone and Day-7 tiles can show small gem amounts. No gem store screen.
