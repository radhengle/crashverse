### 3\. Visual identity

The outpost should have a distinct visual theme:

* Primary colours: Ocean blue, sandstone, dark timber and demon red.

* Secondary colours: Bronze, gold and glowing orange.

* Enemy identity: Demonic skull symbols, red banners and corrupted architecture.

* Environment: Tropical island, turquoise water, distant cliffs, waterfalls and bright skies.

The contrast is intentional: a beautiful sunny island occupied by a terrifying enemy force.

### 7\. Technical asset structure

For Blender-to-Unity production, we planned these separate groups:

```text
TideWatchtower
├── Island_Foundation
├── Stone_Structure
├── Wooden_Supports
├── Main_Tower
├── Roof
├── Left_Archer_Platform
├── Right_Archer_Platform
├── Demon_Banners
├── Corruption_Crystal
├── Defensive_Cannon
├── Decorations
├── Destruction_Parts
└── Water_Interaction
```

Important production rule: The intact tower should be one optimized visual structure, with separate destructible components activated only when damaged. This keeps mobile performance manageable.

My main design intention: Every outpost in CRASHVERSE should have its own architecture, enemy placements, defensive mechanisms and destruction personality. We should never make every level look like the same building with a different colour.

Tide Watchtower establishes that visual standard for Level 1. 🔥
