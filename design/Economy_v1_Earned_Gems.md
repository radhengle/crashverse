# Economy v1: earned-only gems (no real-money purchases in v1)
Owner: Game designer. PM decision: v1 sells nothing for real money, so gems are earned only. Gems and coins still buy cosmetics only (ADD §4); gameplay is never for sale. All values are tunable server data.

## Currencies
- **Coins:** common. Earned from level wins, chests and daily rewards. Spent on coin-priced cosmetics.
- **Gems:** rarer, and earned only. Spent on premium cosmetics and the season premium track.
- **Cosmetic shards:** 10 shards craft a random cosmetic you don't already own.

## Gem sources (an engaged player earns about 150 gems a week)
| Source | Gems |
|---|---|
| Daily login, Day 7 chest | 10 (once a week) |
| Daily Titan completion | 3 a day; +5 for finishing in the top 10% |
| Weekly event, all 5 levels cleared | 15 |
| Weekly event, every level at 3★ | +10 |
| Star milestones per chapter (10/20/30★) | 5 / 10 / 20 (once each) |
| Guardian boss first clear | 25 |
| Season track, every 5th tier | 10 (60 a season) |
| Achievements | 5–25 each |

## Gem sinks (cosmetic only)
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Wrecker skin (epic) | 300 gems |
| Trail or impact VFX skin | 120 gems |
| Avatar frame or title | 60 gems |
| Season premium track unlock | 450 gems (about 3 weeks of play, so you can unlock it within the season you're in) |
| Common skins, decals, decor | Coins only: 500–3,000 |

Targets: a new cosmetic of some kind roughly every week, the premium track within each season for players who play daily, and a first epic skin in about 2 weeks.

## Rules
- There's no gem store, and no gem or currency pack in v1. Store SKUs are hidden until PM enables real-money purchases.
- Gems never buy power-ups, charges, shots, continues, skips, stars, unlocks or Titan attempts.
- Gem balances are server-authoritative. The SG-17 refund and debt rule applies only once real-money gems exist.
